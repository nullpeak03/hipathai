# HiPath AI — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product
HiPath AI is an adaptive learning platform for computer science and technology. It generates personalized roadmaps, teaches lessons, runs quizzes, detects weaknesses, and provides a persistent AI tutor.

## Objectives
- Turn any CS goal into a structured, adaptive roadmap.
- Teach lessons at the learner's level.
- Verify mastery with quizzes and spaced review.
- Provide a context-aware AI tutor.
- Track progress, mastery, and analytics.

## Personas
- Beginner self-learner
- Career switcher
- Student
- Advanced practitioner

## Core Features
1. Onboarding wizard
2. Adaptive roadmap generation
3. Lesson generation
4. Quizzes and phase mastery
5. AI tutor
6. Analytics and gamification
7. Public roadmap library
8. Review and publish workflow

## Success Metrics
- Roadmap completion rate
- Mastery rate (>=90%)
- Quiz pass rate
- Retention and streak
- Time-to-competency

## Non-Goals (V1/V2)
- Multi-tenant teams (V3)
- Paid billing (future)
- Code execution sandbox (future)
